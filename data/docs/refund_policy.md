# Refund policy

Northwind Analytics offers a 30-day refund window for annual subscriptions bought directly from our website. Refund requests made within 30 days of the original charge are approved automatically when the workspace has not exported more than two reports in the previous seven days.

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops future charges, but the current billing month is not refunded.

Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed order form. Refunds for enterprise contracts are handled by the account manager and are not processed through the self-serve billing portal.

Approved refunds appear on the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Chargebacks: when a customer disputes a charge with their bank, the workspace is suspended until the dispute is resolved. Customers should contact the billing team so that evidence can be submitted to the bank.
